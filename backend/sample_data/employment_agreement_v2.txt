EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT (VERSION 2 - REVISED PROPOSAL)

This Employment Agreement is dated as of January 1, 2024, by and between NEXUS CLOUD SOLUTIONS INC. (the "Company"), and JENNIFER ADAMS (the "Executive").

1. POSITION AND RESPONSIBILITIES
The Company hereby employs Executive as Vice President of Global Engineering. Executive shall report directly to the Chief Executive Officer.

2. COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS
(a) Base Salary. The Company shall pay Executive an annualized base salary of $185,000.00, payable semi-monthly in accordance with standard payroll practices.
(b) Vacation. Executive shall be entitled to four (4) weeks of paid vacation per calendar year.
(c) Health Benefits. Executive is eligible for standard medical, dental, and vision insurance.

3. TERM AND TERMINATION
(a) At-Will Employment. Executive's employment is at-will and may be terminated by either party at any time.
(b) Notice Period. Either party shall provide fourteen (14) days' prior written notice of termination without cause.
(c) Severance. In the event Company terminates Executive without cause, Executive shall receive two (2) months of continued base salary as severance, subject to execution of a general release.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY AND IP ASSIGNMENT
Executive agrees to maintain confidentiality of trade secrets and hereby assigns all inventions, patents, and work products developed during employment to the Company.

5. NON-COMPETITION AND NON-SOLICITATION
(a) Non-Solicitation. For twelve (12) months following termination, Executive shall not solicit employees or customers of the Company.
(b) Non-Competition. For twelve (12) months following termination, Executive shall not directly or indirectly engage in, perform services for, or invest in any business competing directly with Company within North America.

6. INDEMNIFICATION BY EXECUTIVE
Executive agrees to defend, indemnify, and hold harmless the Company against any damages, losses, or claims arising from Executive's gross negligence or willful violation of Company policies.

7. GOVERNING LAW AND ARBITRATION
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware. Any dispute shall be resolved through binding arbitration in Wilmington, Delaware.

NEXUS CLOUD SOLUTIONS INC.
By: Mark Thompson, CEO
