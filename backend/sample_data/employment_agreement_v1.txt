EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT (VERSION 1 - ORIGINAL)

This Employment Agreement is dated as of January 1, 2024, by and between NEXUS CLOUD SOLUTIONS INC. (the "Company"), and JENNIFER ADAMS (the "Executive").

1. POSITION AND RESPONSIBILITIES
The Company hereby employs Executive as Senior Director of Engineering. Executive shall report directly to the Chief Technology Officer.

2. COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS
(a) Base Salary. The Company shall pay Executive an annualized base salary of $160,000.00, payable semi-monthly in accordance with standard payroll practices.
(b) Vacation. Executive shall be entitled to three (3) weeks of paid vacation per calendar year.
(c) Health Benefits. Executive is eligible for standard medical, dental, and vision insurance.

3. TERM AND TERMINATION
(a) At-Will Employment. Executive's employment is at-will and may be terminated by either party at any time.
(b) Notice Period. Either party shall provide thirty (30) days' prior written notice of termination without cause.
(c) Severance. In the event Company terminates Executive without cause, Executive shall receive two (2) months of continued base salary as severance.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY
Executive agrees to maintain the confidentiality of all Company proprietary data and trade secrets during and after employment.

5. NON-SOLICITATION
During employment and for six (6) months thereafter, Executive agrees not to solicit employees of the Company.

6. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Washington.

NEXUS CLOUD SOLUTIONS INC.
By: Mark Thompson, CEO
